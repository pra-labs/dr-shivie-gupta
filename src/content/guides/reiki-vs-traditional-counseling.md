## Why Both Modalities Are Essential

1. **Down-Regulating Hyperarousal:** Patients experiencing acute anxiety often cannot engage in rational cognitive reframing because their prefrontal cortex is overwhelmed by cortisol. A 30-minute Reiki biofield reset lowers heart rate, moving the nervous system from fight-or-flight into parasympathetic safety.
2. **Accessing Somatic Emotional Residue:** Many emotional wounds—such as grief or burnout—live in the chest, gut, and throat. Reiki gently unlocks these holding patterns, allowing counseling discussions to proceed with warmth rather than intellectual defensiveness.
3. **Non-Dogmatic Clarity:** Dr. Shivie strictly adheres to professional ethics. The practice incorporates zero fortune telling, astrology, or fear-based dogmas, ensuring a supportive sanctuary for professionals, students, and families across Agra.
