## Step-by-Step Session Walkthrough

### 1. Pre-Session Intake (15 Minutes)
Upon arriving, you will remove your footwear and sit comfortably to enjoy a warm herbal infusion. Dr. Shivie conducts a gentle intake conversation to understand your current physical fatigue, life transitions, sleep quality, and emotional intentions.

### 2. Treatment Table Relaxation (35-45 Minutes)
- **Clothing:** You remain fully clothed throughout the entire duration. Loose-fitting cotton or linen clothing is recommended.
- **Support:** You rest on a cushioned therapy bed supported by orthopedic bolsters, covered by a light, breathable throw.
- **Hand Placements:** Dr. Shivie moves systematically from your crown down through the seven key chakras (head, throat, heart, solar plexus, sacral, root) using either a light, respectful touch or hovering palm placements.

### 3. Common Sensations You May Experience
Every person's energetic constitution responds uniquely:
- Deep thermal sensations (gentle, radiating warmth from the hands)
- Fine vibrational tingling across the fingertips or feet
- Abdominal gurgling (a primary physiological indicator of parasympathetic vagal activation)
- Hypnagogic drift (a restorative theta brainwave state between dreaming and wakefulness)
- Gentle emotional release (unbidden sighs, subtle tears of relief)

### 4. Post-Session Grounding (10 Minutes)
Following the energy work, Dr. Shivie provides a grounding auric sweep, allows you time to slowly return to full awareness, and shares intuitive biofield observations. You will leave with tailored breathing recommendations for home practice.
